Nesvrta LLC Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Nesvrta LLC (“Nesvrta,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at nesvrta.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Notice explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

This Notice covers the Site only. Any mobile applications published by Nesvrta LLC have separate in-app privacy policies that govern data collection within those apps.

Summary

Nesvrta’s website is a static informational site. We do not use analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party embedded content. We do not maintain user accounts, contact forms, or comment systems. The only information we collect is standard technical data automatically recorded by our web server, described below.

Information We Collect

Web server logs

When you visit the Site, our web server automatically records limited technical information required to serve the pages and maintain the security of the Site. This information includes your Internet Protocol (IP) address, the date and time of your request, the pages you accessed, the type of browser and operating system you are using, and, when available, the referring URL.

We do not use cookies to identify or track visitors across sessions or across other websites. Our hosting platform may set strictly necessary session cookies for basic Site functionality; these do not identify you personally and are not used for analytics or advertising.

Information you send us directly

If you contact us using the email addresses published on the Site, we will receive whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use this information only to respond to you.

Information We Do Not Collect

For clarity, we do not:

  • Use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Microsoft Advertising, or any other analytics or advertising service on this Site
  • Operate user accounts, registrations, logins, or profiles on this Site
  • Host contact forms, comment sections, or user-generated content on this Site
  • Process payments on this Site
  • Send marketing or promotional emails from this Site
  • Sell or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes

How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • Deliver the requested Site content to your browser
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Site
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Respond to messages you send us by email
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use this information for marketing, advertising, or profiling.

How Long We Keep Information

Web server logs are retained for approximately 30 days for security and diagnostic purposes and are then deleted or anonymized. Email correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address the subject of the correspondence and for our records.

How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Hosting provider. Our website hosting provider processes web server logs on our behalf as a necessary part of serving the Site. They act as our data processor and are contractually restricted to that purpose.
  • Legal obligations. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nesvrta LLC, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If Nesvrta LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. Any acquirer will be bound by the terms of this Notice or notify you of any material changes.

Your Rights

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland residents

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

The legal basis for processing web server logs is our legitimate interest in maintaining the security and functionality of the Site.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

All users

You may exercise any of these rights, or ask any question about this Notice, by emailing privacy@nesvrta.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

International Users

The Site is operated from the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States. By using the Site, you consent to this transfer.

Children

This Site is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently received information from a child under 13, please contact privacy@nesvrta.com and we will delete it promptly.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through the Site. No method of transmission or storage over the Internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

The Site does not currently link to third-party websites in a way that transfers personal information. If we add such links in the future, we are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy notices.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Effective date” at the top of the Notice. Material changes will be posted prominently on the Site. Your continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Notice.
